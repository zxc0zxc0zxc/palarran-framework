# /palarran:product:acquisition — where the audience comes from

First read [Ahrefs/Airbnb/TikTok cases](cases/acquisition-growth.md), [evidence](evidence.md), [data sources](data-sources.md). Research alternatives' channels and propose a channel test; do not launch campaigns.

For each signal preserve advertiser/domain identity, geography/language/date/window, creative/landing reference, funnel step and classification. Use one table:

| Channel | What to inspect | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Paid search/context | Search intent, advertiser creative, landing/offer, keyword metrics | Spend/CAC/competitor profit |
| Paid TikTok/Meta/video | Library ads, repeated creative themes, disclosed targeting/reach | Incremental sales or budget scale |
| Organic/native creator | Product demonstration, recurring creators, referral links, disclosed sponsorship | That every integration is paid or acquired users for free |
| SEO/content | Job-driven pages, rankings/backlinks/referring domains, tool estimates | Every customer's exact channel/article revenue |
| Referral/partners/community/outbound | Public program terms, partner links, founder distribution | Conversion/revenue without attribution data |

“Native advertising” describes a format; organic/paid describes payment/distribution: one UGC video can combine creator sponsorship, an organic post and Spark amplification. Label known/unknown sponsorship rather than guessing from style. Brand search may result from other channels; direct traffic is not a loyal audience. No ad in the selected library/region/window does not prove no advertising.

For each alternative diagram the observed/hypothesized acquisition→landing→activation→payment→retention funnel; mark unavailable transitions dashed/unknown. Never infer budget from creative counts or lifetime ads, or treat longevity as proof of profitability. [Google Ads Transparency](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ads/announcing-the-launch-of-the-new-Ads-Transparency-Center/) supports observed ad signals, not competitor P&L.

Your proposal: audience/job/offer, creative angle, channel fit, required landing/onboarding capability, unit-economics constraints from the revenue report. Experiment: H-ID, eligible unit/cohort, primary metric and denominator, attribution window/dedup rules, guardrails/retention/refunds, proposed spend+time cap/stop, owner/data access. Views/CTR/CPI supplement, not replace, activated/paying retained users. Organic cost includes production and people's time. Platform ROAS differs from blended economics/incrementality; separate observed correlation from controlled lift.

DoD: evidence table, a reasoned channel shortlist for stage/region/margin, competing explanations and future test cards; no automatic ad purchase/outreach/pixel installation. Output using [channels](../../templates/product/channels.md).
