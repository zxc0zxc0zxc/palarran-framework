The historical [Datakit design](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/blob/b5d2db5f178263fac27d914cc4ecc1bc8eba31ab/docs/design/2355_datakit.md) is explicitly a proposal; implementation evidence comes from the pinned source files. Upstream blog scale/speed claims remain author reports. No upstream throughput, quality or cost improvement is claimed for Palarran. Exact-payload matching is narrower than Marin's word n-gram decontamination and cannot detect paraphrases or embedded answer substrings.
