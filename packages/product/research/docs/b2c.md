# B2C: audience and inexpensive acquisition

First read [go-to-market](go-to-market.md), [Russian and other-market cases](cases/b2c-frugal-growth.md), [prices](pricing.md) and the [registry](go-to-market-sources.md). Optimize access to the first relevant users and paying customers, not attractive reach figures.

## Audience before development

Define a narrow segment: geography/language, situation/job, current substitute, trigger, user/payer, expected frequency/price/retention. Where do these people already seek a solution, and whom do they trust? Support with sources/observed behavior; “all young people on TikTok” is not a segment. Clarify unknown monetization/geography; never invent LTV. Prepare an offer and short CTA matching the actual product stage. Before build, plan problem/interest testing, but do not promise a finished product or collect payments through this skill.

## Channel search order

1. Cash-free options: owned content/demonstrations, niche communities under their rules, founder-accessible partnerships/referrals, useful material addressing audience queries. Verify access and moderation; free access does not guarantee distribution. SEO has lead time and labor costs.
2. Native creator/TikTok/short video: small relevant creators and local audiences, proposed barter/collaborative content. This is a **deal hypothesis** without published terms/a quote. Include goods cost, delivery, filming, editing, rights and time. Historical BuduFood illustrates a mechanism, not a current rate.
3. Telegram: specific topical channels/posts and public media kits, creator audience, format/duration/deletion/disclosure/fees. Check median comparable post views, geography/topic fit, suspicious spikes and data limitations; subscribers are not guaranteed reach. A native post, direct booking and Telegram Ads are different products/prices.
4. Paid search/social only when the minimum **complete** test fits the actual cap, is geographically available and has verifiable measurement. Do not allocate budget by habit. Reject high prices immediately instead of building a campaign around them.

Research a Russian and an international startup case; explain audience, stage, platform and cost applicability. Do not scale mature Flowwow or vendor-selected success KPIs to a new product. Native is not organic: an advertising integration remains paid even if it resembles an ordinary video.

## Concrete shortlist and tests

For at most 2–3 best candidates: audience and observed fit, URL of a specific platform/creator or justified search-intent group, format/creative/offer/CTA, quote and date or unknown, full cash/time costs, existing access/eligibility, primary metric, denominator/window, attribution/dedup, cap/stop and owner. Never fill a quota with paid channels when cash cap=0. Unknown price/placement rights stays conditional; without any accessible test, honestly report needs_input.

Do not promise CPM/CAC from a public rate card. Known price / observed comparable views gives scenario CPM, not a guarantee; views → visits → activation → paid → retained requires separate measurements. CAC uses new paying customers with an explicit cost set; beforehand only proposed targets/sensitivity are valid, not actual CAC. Unknown margin/retention makes economic readiness conditional.

Stop at the cap, irrelevant audience, unavailable measurement or failed activation guardrail. Exact thresholds are our reasoned proposals, not startup facts.
