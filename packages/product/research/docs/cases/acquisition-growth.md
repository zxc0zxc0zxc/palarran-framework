# Acquisition: mechanisms and boundaries of success

## Ahrefs: product-led content

[Ahrefs describes its approach](https://ahrefs.com/blog/product-led-content/): useful material demonstrates solving a task with the product. Transfer job/intent-driven content topics, natural product demonstrations and activation/paid-outcome tracking. This is the channel owner's account; incremental CAC/control groups are undisclosed. SEO requires time and editorial resources; organic does not mean free. Do not transfer mature domain authority to a new site.

## Airbnb: founder distribution

[Airbnb's early YC experience](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/the-airbnbs/) reminds us that the first useful users may require manual work. Transfer a narrow segment and fast feedback, including founder time in costs. This does not prove a scalable referral/paid loop; a successful company has many other contributing factors. Outreach remains a future experiment with explicit scope.

## SweetyTreatyCo: organic creative → paid Spark Ads

On the last page of [TikTok Formula 4 Success](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_Formula_4_Success.pdf), the platform describes an SMB e-commerce business using existing organic content in Spark Ads, configuring pixel/events and optimizing for Complete Payment. The platform claims improved results; this is a vendor-selected success story, not an independent incrementality test. Numerical KPIs from the graphic are not transcribed here; raw economics/control data was not supplied.

Transfer separate accounting for organic, creator sponsorship and paid amplification of one video, conversion deduplication, and payment/retention tests instead of views. E-commerce and SaaS have different margins/purchase cycles; transferring CPA without this context is invalid. Pixel setup/ad launches are outside the research skill.

## What not to copy

No case establishes a universal best channel or budget. Freeze population, primary metric, guardrails, duration/sample rationale and stop condition before your experiments: [Microsoft experiment patterns](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/patterns-of-trustworthy-experimentation-pre-experiment-stage/). This adapts measurement principles; interviews/concierge/bounded pilots may fit small samples better than a weak A/B. Contract: [acquisition](../acquisition.md).
