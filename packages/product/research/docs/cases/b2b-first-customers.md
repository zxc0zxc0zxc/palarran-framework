# B2B cases: the first small buyers

These are first-person/company accounts, not independent causal studies. [Dates and applicability](../go-to-market-sources.md). Small-only is the user's scope choice; these cases do not prove small customers are always easier or more profitable.

## Yclients: from a specific salon to the first buyers

The [founder interview on RB.RU](https://rb.ru/interview/yclients/) describes an owned salon, a prototype, lack of immediate demand and objections about data/control. It names two family salons found at a Yandex conference as the first customer. This historical early-stage account is neither a current rate card nor representative sales statistics.

Transfer a narrow real buyer/job, owner access, trust/data questions, manual onboarding and a bounded pilot. Do not transfer lifetime free support: account for cost to serve. Retrospectively, the founder thinks chains could have been approached earlier; preserve this contradiction rather than rewriting it as “small is always better.” Our small filter serves the current scope, not a claimed Yclients finding. CAC, reply rate and the conference's causal effect are undisclosed. Our test: conversations about the current process and agreement to a bounded pilot; a company list is no substitute.

## YouGile: cases as useful access to an audience

The [company news about its case video blog](https://ru.yougile.com/news/2020-11-11_new_year_plans.html) describes regular product-use videos and feedback, including manufacturing tasks. Transfer a concrete scenario/demonstration in owned distribution rather than generic feature advertising. The publication does not disclose attributable CAC/sales; production labor still exists. Do not use mature large-company customers as small-fit prospects. Test relevant inquiries/activations from a case, not view counts.

## Buffer: test interest and the offer before lengthy development

The [founder's account of the early years](https://buffer.com/resources/10-years/) describes an early landing-page test and simple first version; the first paying customer came four days **after launch**, not after the first tweet. Transfer a small value/price test before expanding scope and a short path to the outcome. Historical SaaS does not supply current CAC/conversion or prove channels for our market. No replies calls for segment/offer review; do not build indefinitely instead of testing.

## Practical adaptation

For each transfer record case/source E-ID → similar stage/buyer → what not to transfer → cheap falsifying test. Assumptions about a specific company's pain are not customer interviews. First-customer review checks size/ownership, an accessible path, bounded offer and economics; actual sales remain not_run.
