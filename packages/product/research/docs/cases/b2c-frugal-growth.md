# B2C cases: Russian and international channels

[Source registry](../go-to-market-sources.md). Success in an article and an author's claim are not causal proof; transfer a mechanism for a bounded test, not promised conversion.

## BuduFood / Just Up: local TikTok and barter

In a [practical biz360 interview](https://biz360.ru/materials/zakhodit-v-tiktok-nado-bylo-eshchye-vchera-kak-nachat-prodvizhenie-v-servise-korotkikh-video/), an agency cofounder describes one BuduFood location in Barnaul: owned entertaining backstage content and local creator integrations exchanged for delivered food. The format was tailored to the creator. Awareness/clicks are claimed; attributable paying CAC/retention is undisclosed. The author has an interest in promoting agency services.

Transfer local audience fit, a native demonstration and a small creator-fit test. Barter has product, delivery and production costs; do not call it free. Historical agency prices and Instagram traffic are not a current recommendation for Russia. Separately verify current platform/geography availability and whether the advertising path is permitted. This skill does not proceed to creator contact.

## HireSeeker: organic traffic and late discovery's cost

The [first-person ProductRadar account](https://productradar.ru/blog/kak-ya-sdelal-agregator-vakansij-hireseeker/) describes six months of development without showing people, followed by a plateau. The founder names contextual LinkedIn comments/offers and organic search as primary sources, estimating organic share “by feel.” This is the author's channel testimony, not measured attribution; we **do not accept the PMF claim as confirmed**.

Transfer early audience-access testing before a lengthy build; topical help and search intent are hypotheses for our product. Do not transfer unverified traffic shares or automatically authorize spam/comments. Jobseekers are users; separately establish the actual payer/monetization for our B2C/B2B2C.

## SweetyTreatyCo: an international SMB and organic → paid

The [official TikTok Formula 4 Success](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_Formula_4_Success.pdf) presents an e-commerce case with organic content, Spark amplification and a Complete Payment event. This platform-selected account is not an independent experiment. Transfer creative-relevance testing before paid amplification and payment measurement instead of views. Do not copy graphic KPIs or an undisclosed budget; paid amplification requires our caps/eligibility/measurement. With cash cap=0, only organic-approach research remains.

## Flowwow: a useful mechanism, an unsuitable budget

The [Mindbox interview, 2022-09-11](https://mindbox.ru/journal/experts/flowwow/) describes an already large marketplace and substantial influencer spending. Do not use this scale as a startup's cheap-first plan. Creator fit and attribution may be discussed while distinguishing self-reported revenue from incremental profit; exclude expensive integrations until detailed. This is a counterexample to transferring a mature business, not another mandatory paid channel.

## What to test here

Narrow segment/geography/job → specific platform → honest full cost → proposed experiment with outcome/denominator/window/cap/stop. A fresh public quote outweighs historical “cheap.” Never invent a missing quote; free/barter does not replace time accounting. If paid does not fit, do not include it merely to diversify options.
