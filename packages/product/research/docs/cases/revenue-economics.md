# Revenue and economics: Buffer's open data

[Open Buffer](https://buffer.com/open) publishes MRR/ARR/ARPU; the [annual report 2023](https://buffer.com/shareholders/2023) explains the relationship between paying customers and monthly revenue per customer, and business changes. These are company disclosures, not an independent audit or proof of complete unit economics.

Do not copy constantly changing dashboard totals into a reusable case. In a specific study preserve metric date, definition, period, paid-account/user unit and link. If the dashboard data date is invisible, separately record retrieval date and unknown as-of. Check rounding and coverage rather than treating an approximate difference between ARR and 12×MRR as a contradiction.

Our adaptation: seek direct disclosures before models and reconstruct an economic bridge from comparable data. `MRR = paid_accounts × monthly_ARPA` requires the same base, currency and period. MAU is not paid_accounts. ARR is an annualized run rate, not automatically recognized revenue for the previous year. New MRR growth does not guarantee positive cash flow.

For a private competitor without disclosures, honest “revenue unknown” is acceptable. Public traffic does not reveal paid base, churn, discounts or enterprise/offline sales. Our model's range is a scenario, not a company fact. Buffer shows transparency is possible; it does not authorize transferring its conversion/ARPA to other products.
