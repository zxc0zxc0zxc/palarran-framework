## Unavailable checks

Official [FAS](https://fas.gov.ru/documents/690033) and [ERIR](https://erir.grfc.ru/) could not be read with the research tool (timeout 2026-10-19). This is **not a legal opinion**. Do not draw legal conclusions from search snippets; before an executable paid plan check current eligibility/requirements through available official sources, otherwise conditional. Requiring this current review is our procedure.

Recommendations about channel order, small-size default, batch size, budget policy and checkpoints are our adaptation to user constraints, not statements by the listed companies. Example caps are scenarios; unknown prices/metrics are never filled with guesses.
