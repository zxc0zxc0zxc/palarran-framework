## Domain work

Read Ahrefs/Airbnb/SweetyTreatyCo cases and check stage/business-model fit. Gather per-alternative observed paid-search/social, organic-content, creator/native, referral/partner/community signals: advertiser/domain identity, geography/language/date, creative/landing, delivery/sponsorship classification and coverage. Native format is not organic: creator posts may combine sponsorship/paid amplification; unknown payment stays unknown.

Ad libraries do not reveal complete P&L; ad counts/duration/views/reach prove neither spend/CAC/ROAS nor retained customers. Missing archive entries do not prove absent channels. Diagram observed/hypothesized acquisition→activation→payment→retention with unknown edges. Produce stage-aware shortlist/future test cards: job/offer/creative/channel, eligible unit, primary outcome/denominator, attribution window/dedup, guardrails, sample/time/cost rationale, cap/stop/owner. Include production/founder/creator/ad costs and margin/retention. No campaigns/pixels/creator messages.

## Verification, decisions and handoff

Bind every material claim/number to E-ID, primary URL, publisher/publication/retrieval/as-of/period/geography/unit/method/limits and reasoned confidence. Distinguish observed/disclosed/estimated/hypothesis/scenario/unknown; self-reports/vendor stories are not causal evidence. Never invent interviews, traffic, revenue, conversion/ROAS or data charts. Preserve contradictions; check common measurement bases and successful-case applicability.

Before dependent decisions record concise rationale/evidence/alternatives/consequences in target `.palarran/product/<task>/decisions/`; update shared INDEX, reuses/supersedes/history. Results belong in `revisions/vNNN/` with exact source references; product INDEX catalogs reports, not duplicate ADRs. Never overwrite others' existing tasks/history; source changes flag downstream references needing review.

Check domain DoD. Report status draft/research_complete/needs_input/blocked; experiments stay not_run until actual evidence. Research complete is not PMF/market validated/build-ready. Conclude briefly with findings, basis, unknowns/blockers, next cheap falsifying test and report/ledger/ADR/handoff links. No automatic execution transition.
